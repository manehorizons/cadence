## Carry-forward gotchas

- **`cadence draft new`/`cadence spec new` silently truncate multi-line text when seeding from an approved SPEC.** Both `parseSpecMd` and `parseDraftMd` extract only the FIRST LINE of the Objective (`.split('\n')[0]`) and only the FIRST LINE of each AC's Given/When/Then (regex `.+` without the `/s` flag). If you scaffold a new DRAFT and its Objective/ACs look suspiciously short/cut-off mid-sentence, that's this bug (`rec-20260704-002`), not a mistake in the SPEC. Always diff the seeded DRAFT against the approved SPEC.md and hand-restore the full text before filling in Tasks/Boundaries — don't approve a DRAFT with truncated ACs.
