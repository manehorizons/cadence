# 202-01 — Team rollout kit

## Objective

Give teams (not just solo operators) a concrete way to adopt CADENCE in CI/PR review — a rollout kit that normalizes SUMMARY artifacts for reviewers and documents how a team enforces or inspects CADENCE gate results without CADENCE replacing existing CI or human review.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-1: PR reviewers can see gate/settle results without reading raw JSON
Given a phase has been settled and its SUMMARY.json/SUMMARY.md committed
When a reviewer runs a new `cadence summary render` (or similarly named) subcommand against that phase's SUMMARY.json
Then it prints a deterministic, human-readable rendering of gate outcomes and per-AC status suitable for pasting into a PR description or comment, without needing to open `.cadence/phases/*/SUMMARY.json` by hand

### AC-2: A documented rollout path exists for adopting CADENCE as a team, not just solo
Given a team wants to start using CADENCE across multiple contributors
When they follow the rollout documentation
Then they can find guidance covering shared conventions (PR template, what reviewers should check, how gate bypasses are surfaced to the team) that does not require replacing their existing CI or code-review process

## Constraints

- Must not replace or duplicate existing CI — this augments PR review, it does not gate merges on its own.
- Must not require every reviewer to understand CADENCE internals (loop, gates, phases) to get value from the summary.
- Documentation-heavy phase — spec/doc drift risk (per milestone pre-mortem); keep claims verifiable against actual CLI/output behavior, matching this repo's doc-sync conventions.
- Out of scope: `cadence init --ci` / CI-gate-workflow scaffolding for consumer repos (rec-20260709-003) — that stays a separate future phase.
